Sirib — Repair & Service Domain PRD

Status: Revised implementation PRDTarget: Rails 8 + PostgreSQL, directly portable to Elixir/Phoenix + EctoInitial vertical: Cellphone sales & repairFuture verticals: Computer, motorcycle, automobile, and other service businesses

1. Objective

Build a repair/service domain that supports customer-owned physical items without Rails polymorphic associations, STI, or JSONB for core business data.

The same WorkOrder workflow must support:

cellphone repair

computer repair

motorcycle repair

automobile repair

future serviceable equipment

The core relationship is:

Customer
  └── ServiceAsset
        └── WorkOrder
              ├── WorkOrderTask
              ├── WorkOrderPart
              ├── WorkOrderService
              └── Charge

ServiceAssetCategory defines the type and fields of a service asset.

They are business data.

Do not initially build an arbitrary customer-defined metadata system. Sirib should own the initial category definitions.

10. Customer ↔ ServiceAsset

A customer has many service assets.

Customer
  has_many :service_assets

A work order references both the customer and service asset:

WorkOrder
  customer_id
  service_asset_id

The application/domain layer must enforce:

work_order.customer_id
==
work_order.service_asset.customer_id

This explicit customer reference is intentional for reporting, authorization, and historical transaction queries.

If a different person later drops off an asset, introduce a separate Requester concept rather than changing the meaning of Customer.

15. Inventory Integration

Repair parts must integrate with the existing inventory domain.

The repair domain must not silently mutate stock.

Correct flow:

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓
Stock
   ↓
Inventory movement
   ↓
LineItem
type = internal_use

Example:

WorkOrder #5001

1 × Brake Pad
2 × Engine Oil

The work order records the usage.

The inventory domain records the resulting movement.

This preserves auditability.

24. Database Integrity

Use explicit foreign keys.

Important constraints:

service_asset_values:
  UNIQUE(service_asset_id, service_asset_field_id)

Tenant/business consistency must be enforced.

A Business A record must not reference:

Business B customer

Business B service asset

Business B category

Business B store location

Business B product

Business B stock

Use database constraints where practical and application/domain validation where composite tenant constraints are not practical.

One important design decision

I would not automatically consider every accessory attached to the vehicle a ServiceItem.

For example, factory-installed:

ServiceItem is specifically for items received alongside the asset and tracked for return.

That's a very clean domain boundary and works equally well for phones, computers, motorcycles, and automobiles.
